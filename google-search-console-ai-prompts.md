# Google Search Console AI Prompts

Use these prompts in the AI prompt box in Google Search Console to uncover SEO opportunities. The exact results depend on the data available in your property and how the feature interprets each prompt.

## 1. Find hidden opportunities
**Prompt**
> Show me my hidden opportunities.

**What it helps with**
Find queries that receive impressions but relatively few clicks. Review the results for relevant pages where stronger titles, meta descriptions, search intent alignment, or content improvements could help.

## 2. Find low-hanging fruit keywords
**Prompt**
> Find my low-hanging fruit keywords.

**What it helps with**
Identify queries that may be close to stronger rankings. As a follow-up, review queries ranking around positions 4–20 and prioritise relevant terms where improving the page could realistically make a difference.

## 3. Find question-based searches
**Prompt**
> Show me queries containing what or how.

**What it helps with**
Find question-style searches people use when looking for information related to your business. These queries can inspire FAQs, supporting content, and improvements to existing pages.

## More useful Google Search Console prompts

### 4. Find pages with high impressions and low CTR
**Prompt**
> Show me pages with high impressions and a low click-through rate.

**Use it for:** Finding pages that may benefit from clearer titles and meta descriptions, while checking that the page matches the search intent.

### 5. Find queries where average position is improving
**Prompt**
> Show me queries whose average position has improved over the last three months.

**Use it for:** Spotting keywords that are gaining visibility and may be worth supporting with relevant content and internal links.

### 6. Find queries losing visibility
**Prompt**
> Show me queries that have lost clicks or impressions compared with the previous three months.

**Use it for:** Finding potential declines to investigate. Check seasonality, demand, ranking changes, indexing, and competing pages before deciding what to fix.

### 7. Find pages ranking for many queries
**Prompt**
> Show me pages that rank for the most queries and summarise their main search themes.

**Use it for:** Understanding which pages have broad visibility and where relevant content improvements may help.

### 8. Find branded versus non-branded searches
**Prompt**
> Compare my branded and non-branded search performance.

**Use it for:** Understanding how much organic visibility comes from people already searching for your brand versus people discovering you through other searches.

### 9. Find device performance differences
**Prompt**
> Compare my search performance on mobile and desktop, and highlight the biggest differences.

**Use it for:** Finding device-specific differences worth investigating, especially when clicks or CTR vary noticeably.

### 10. Find country-level opportunities
**Prompt**
> Show me the countries generating the most impressions but relatively few clicks.

**Use it for:** Exploring international search visibility. Only prioritise a country if it is relevant to your audience and business.

### 11. Find pages with declining clicks
**Prompt**
> Which pages have had the biggest drop in clicks compared with the previous three months?

**Use it for:** Creating a shortlist of pages to investigate for ranking losses, seasonality, changes in search demand, or technical issues.

### 12. Find queries with strong CTR
**Prompt**
> Show me queries with a high click-through rate and explain which pages receive those clicks.

**Use it for:** Learning which searches and page combinations attract clicks, then applying relevant insights to similar pages.

### 13. Find content ideas from search queries
**Prompt**
> Group my search queries by topic and suggest content ideas based on relevant queries with impressions.

**Use it for:** Discovering themes for new articles, FAQs, and service-page improvements. Validate each idea against your audience and business goals.

### 14. Find pages that may need better content alignment
**Prompt**
> Find queries where my page receives impressions but may not closely match the search intent.

**Use it for:** Reviewing whether the page answers the question or fulfils the need behind the search. Treat the output as a shortlist to check manually.

### 15. Compare performance by date
**Prompt**
> Compare clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position for the last 28 days with the previous 28 days.

**Use it for:** Getting a quick performance comparison. Check that the selected date ranges are comparable and allow for reporting delays.

### 16. Find internal linking opportunities
**Prompt**
> Based on my top-performing pages and pages with relevant search queries, suggest internal linking opportunities.

**Use it for:** Finding possible links between related pages. Check each suggested source and destination manually before adding links.

## How to use the results

1. Open Google Search Console and select the correct property.
2. Enter a prompt in the AI prompt box, if it is available in your account.
3. Review the response and verify important findings in the Performance report.
4. Prioritise opportunities based on relevance, business value, search intent, and the effort needed to improve the page.

**Important:** These are natural-language prompt ideas, not guaranteed commands. The AI prompt feature may interpret them differently, and availability can vary by account or interface. Verify the results in Search Console before making SEO decisions.
